How are training programs delivered?

Our learning support includes live training, recorded sessions, doubt sessions, practical exercises, and test and interview preparation. Confirm batch timings and delivery arrangements with our team.

Do you provide placement support?

Yes, we provide placement support, including preparation and guidance. However, we do not guarantee employment.

How do I apply for a paid internship?

Choose a domain and duration in the separate internship application form. Applicant fees are ₹499 for 1 month, ₹999 for 2 months, ₹1,299 for 3 months and ₹1,999 for 6 months. Submit your transaction reference and receipt for team verification. These amounts are fees, not a stipend or salary. Confirm availability and terms with the team.

Apply for Paid Internship
What support is included in an internship?

Internship support includes monthly tasks, monthly assessments, learning notes and project support. An internship certificate and experience letter are provided after the agreed completion requirements are met.

Will I receive a certificate?

Training and internship certificates document completed experiences. Confirm the relevant attendance, assignment and completion requirements before enrolling. The certificate shown on the website is a demo specimen.
